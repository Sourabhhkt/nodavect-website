Nodavect

Optimization for infrastructure planning

Nodavect develops decision-support tools that help organizations evaluate infrastructure investments and operating strategies using mathematical optimization.

Produced Water Infrastructure

Our initial focus is produced-water network planning. Nodavect helps evaluate pipeline, treatment, storage, disposal, and water-routing decisions across interconnected systems.

What We Do

Using projected water volumes, existing infrastructure, candidate projects, capacities, and costs, our optimization models identify feasible infrastructure and operating plans designed to reduce total system cost.

Pilot Projects

We are currently working with industry partners to evaluate the technology on real and historical infrastructure-planning cases.

About

Nodavect was founded by Sourabh Choudhary, PhD, whose research at Georgia Tech focused on optimization methods for water-network design and operation.
